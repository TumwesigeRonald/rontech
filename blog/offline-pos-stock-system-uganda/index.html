Top Offline Retail POS & Stock Management System for Businesses in Uganda

Power cuts and unstable internet are part of daily trading. If your point of sale stops working when the connection drops, you lose sales and your stock records fall out of step with the shelf. For shops and supermarkets in Kampala and across Uganda, offline-first retail POS software solves that.

What offline-first means

The till keeps working with no internet. Sales, receipts and stock movements are saved on the device and then synchronised to the central system when the connection returns, so nothing is lost and nothing is entered twice.

What a good stock management system should do

  • Reduce stock automatically with every sale, and add to it when new stock is received
  • Warn you when items fall below a reorder level
  • Track stock per branch if you run more than one outlet
  • Show which products sell fastest and which sit on the shelf
  • Support mobile money and cash payments with clear end-of-day totals

Control over cashiers

Give each cashier their own login, record who made each sale, and restrict sensitive actions such as discounts, voids and price changes to a manager. At close of day the cash count can be compared against what the system expects.

Choosing custom or off-the-shelf

Off-the-shelf retail POS software is quick to start. Custom software makes sense when your process is unusual, you need your own reports, or you want it linked to WhatsApp, mobile money or an online shop. Either way, test the offline behaviour with the internet switched off before you commit.
