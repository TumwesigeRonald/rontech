AI Agents for Small Business: Automating Daily Admin & WhatsApp Receipts

Most small businesses lose hours every week to the same small jobs: checking what is running low, writing receipts, chasing payments and answering the same customer questions. These are the jobs lightweight automation does well.

Three workflows worth starting with

  • Stock alerts: when an item in your point of sale drops below a set level, you get a message so you reorder before it runs out
  • Invoices and receipts: a sale or order creates a clean invoice or receipt automatically, ready to print or send
  • Customer updates on WhatsApp: order ready, payment received or balance due, sent from a template instead of typed one by one

If your customers already use WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business integration lets these messages go out from your own system, and a custom inventory system can feed it with live stock data.

Where AI fits in

An AI assistant can draft replies to common customer questions, summarise the day's sales or turn a rough message into a tidy invoice line. It should suggest, and a person should approve anything involving money or a promise to a customer.

Start small

Pick the one task that eats the most time, automate it, and check it for a few weeks before adding another. Automations that are simple and understood by your staff last longer than clever ones nobody trusts.
