Engineering High-Performance School Portals with Node.js & PostgreSQL

A school portal is quiet for weeks and then, on results day, hundreds of parents and students open it at the same moment. A system built for average traffic will slow down or fail exactly when people care most.

Why Node.js and PostgreSQL

Node.js handles many simultaneous connections efficiently, which suits a portal where most requests are short reads like "show my child's results". PostgreSQL is a reliable relational database with strong guarantees, which is what you want for marks, attendance and fees.

Handling the spike

  • Serverless hosting: capacity scales up when traffic arrives and back down afterwards, so you do not pay for idle servers
  • Database indexing: indexes on the columns used in lookups, such as student, class, term and subject, keep queries fast as the data grows
  • Connection pooling: a pooled connection layer stops thousands of requests from overwhelming the database
  • Caching: results that do not change after publishing can be served from a cache instead of recalculated

Test before results day

Run a load test with realistic numbers before release. It is far cheaper to find a slow query in a test than to find it while parents are waiting.
