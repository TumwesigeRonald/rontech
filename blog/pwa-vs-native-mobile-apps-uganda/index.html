Why Ugandan Businesses Need Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) over Expensive Mobile Apps

Many business owners are told they need a mobile app, then quoted thousands of dollars for separate Android and iPhone versions. For most local businesses there is a cheaper route that works just as well for customers: a Progressive Web App.

What is a PWA?

A PWA is a website that behaves like an app. Customers open it in their browser, tap "Add to Home screen", and it sits on their phone with its own icon and opens full screen. There is nothing to download from an app store.

What a PWA can do

  • Work offline or on a weak connection by saving key pages and data on the phone
  • Send push notifications on Android, and on iPhone once the app is added to the Home screen
  • Load fast and use very little data, which matters when bundles are expensive
  • Update instantly for everyone, with no waiting for store approval

When you still need a native app

Native apps are worth the cost when you need deep phone features such as advanced Bluetooth, heavy background processing or demanding graphics. A shop catalogue, a booking system, a school parent portal or a staff dashboard rarely does.

We design Progressive Web Apps and custom web portals for Ugandan businesses, schools and organisations.

The cost difference

A native app means building and maintaining two versions, paying store fees and going through reviews on every update. A PWA is one codebase that serves every phone and the desktop. For most small organisations that is the difference between a project that fits the budget and one that does not.
